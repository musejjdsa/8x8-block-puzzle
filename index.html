How to play

  • Drag a piece onto the board — or tap a piece, then tap where it should go. Pieces can't be rotated.
  • Fill all 8 cells in a row or a column to clear it. Clearing on consecutive moves builds your combo multiplier.
  • Empty the whole board with a placement for a Perfect Clear: +500 × multiplier.
  • The game ends when none of your 3 pieces fits anywhere.
